Dynamic Trade, Spatial Macroeconomics & Geoeconomics

My research lies at the intersection of international trade and macroeconomics. I bring the dynamics of growth emphasized in macroeconomics into quantitative trade models, use spatial and micro data to study adjustment across places and firms, and examine how geopolitical power and fragmentation reshape trade policy, market access, and welfare.

Trade · Growth · Innovation · WTO Essay Award 2024

Trade, Growth, and Product Innovation

How much of the gains from trade come from the creation of new products?

EU integration increased market access, product entry, R&D, and trade. I develop a dynamic general-equilibrium model combining Eaton–Kortum trade with Romer-style innovation to quantify static and dynamic gains from integration.

PaperAward
Market access → innovation → growth
02
Dynamic Trade · Supply Chains

Dynamic Adjustment to Trade Shocks

with Junyuan Chen, Marc Muendler, and Fabian Trottner

Why does trade adjust slowly after shocks?

A model of staggered sourcing decisions rationalizes the gap between short- and long-run trade elasticities. Adjustment frictions substantially amplify welfare losses from the US–China trade war.

Paper
Sourcing frictions shape the path of adjustment
03
Trade Policy · Geoeconomics

Trade Policy and Power Politics

with Philip Economides and Freddie Papazyan

How does geopolitical power change optimal trade policy?

Cross-country evidence on military power and tariffs motivates a quantitative trade model in which geopolitical alignment, coercive power, and conflict outside options shape tariff choices.

Paper
Power changes both tariffs and the scope for cooperation
